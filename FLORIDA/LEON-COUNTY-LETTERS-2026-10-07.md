# Leon County letters — send-ready, October 7, 2026

Redrafted October 7, 2026. The September 23 drafts (`leon-county-followup-P022996.txt`, `sao2-records-request-hrs-1993.txt`) were never saved to the archive, so these replace them. Facts re-verified the same day against the Tampa Bay Times archive:

- Indictments returned Wednesday, August 18, 1993, by an 18-member grand jury; Circuit Judge L. Ralph Smith presiding; State Attorney Willie Meggs led the investigation. Viann Hardy, HRS deputy secretary: four indictments, seven felony counts. Judy Mitchell, FLORIDA project director, also indicted. ([Tampa Bay Times, Aug. 19, 1993](https://www.tampabay.com/archive/1993/08/19/former-hrs-officials-indicted/))
- Objections to the presentment heard in closed session before Circuit Judge Philip Padovano, September 1993. ([Tampa Bay Times, Sept. 8, 1993](https://www.tampabay.com/archive/1993/09/08/media-access-to-computer-hearing-denied/))
- Hardy's charges dropped; Mitchell convicted May 25, 1994 of two misdemeanor counts of false official statements, acquitted of two felony counts; Judge Padovano presiding; Deputy State Attorney Tony Guarisco prosecuted. ([Tampa Bay Times, May 26, 1994](https://www.tampabay.com/archive/1994/05/26/ex-computer-chief-receives-a-split-verdict/))

**Correction carried forward.** The August 20 request said a circuit judge "set the case aside" after Mitchell's conviction. The press record shows only that defense counsel planned to renew a motion for acquittal; the outcome is not established. These letters do not repeat the claim.

## Letter 2 — Office of the State Attorney, Second Judicial Circuit (new request)

**To:** sao2publicrecordsrequests@leoncountyfl.gov

**Subject:** `Public records request — 1993 HRS grand jury: State v. Viann Hardy and State v. Judy Mitchell`

```
Dear Public Records Custodian,

Under Chapter 119, Florida Statutes, I request copies of the following records of the Office of the State Attorney, Second Judicial Circuit.

Background. In 1992–93 a Leon County grand jury, led by State Attorney Willie Meggs and presided over by Circuit Judge L. Ralph Smith, investigated the Department of Health and Rehabilitative Services' FLORIDA public-assistance computer system. On August 18, 1993 it returned indictments against two former HRS administrators, Viann Hardy and Judy Mitchell, together with a presentment. The State later dropped the charges against Ms. Hardy. Ms. Mitchell was tried before Circuit Judge Philip J. Padovano and found guilty on May 25, 1994 of two misdemeanor counts; Deputy State Attorney Tony Guarisco prosecuted.

Records requested:

1. The grand jury presentment returned on or about August 18, 1993 concerning HRS and the FLORIDA system, in the form in which it was ultimately made public, together with any order resolving objections filed under section 905.28, Florida Statutes.

2. The office's closed case files for State v. Viann Hardy and State v. Judy Mitchell, including case numbers, charging documents, the nolle prosequi or other disposition for Ms. Hardy, and the verdict, judgment, and sentence for Ms. Mitchell.

3. Any exhibits, reports, or documents in the office's files that were obtained from HRS or EDS Federal Corporation concerning the FLORIDA system, including contract, payment-authorization, and capacity or performance records.

What I am not requesting. I do not request grand jury testimony or any material made confidential by sections 905.27 or 905.395, Florida Statutes. Where a record contains exempt material, please redact it, identify the statutory basis for each redaction, and provide the remainder.

If records no longer exist. If any of these files were destroyed under a retention schedule or transferred to the State Archives of Florida, please tell me in writing, with the schedule item, disposition date, or accession number if available. That answer fully satisfies the item.

Format and cost. Electronic copies by OneDrive link are entirely satisfactory. I have reviewed your published fee schedule. Please send an estimate before beginning work; I authorize charges of up to $100 without further approval.

I am a technology industry analyst conducting historical research for publication on the FLORIDA system.

Thank you for your assistance.

Peter Kastner
20 Seawatch Drive, Savannah, GA 31411-2600
pskastner@gmail.com
```
