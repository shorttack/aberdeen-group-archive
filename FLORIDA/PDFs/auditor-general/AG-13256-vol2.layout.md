      y          During the audit period, the FDOT made one land exchange in which two State-owned
                 parcels with settlement values totaling $564,600 and State funds of $94,500 were
                 exchanged for four right-of-way parcels with settlement values totaling $659,100. In
                 our review of this exchange, we noted that the FDOT allocated the amount of the cash
                 payment ($94,500) to the four right-of-way parcels based on the complexity of the
                 appraisal process and not on the basis of the relative settlement values of the four
                 parcels. The allocation of the cash payment, on a basis other than relative settlement
                 values, resulted in the FDOT overbilling the FHWA by $26,978.

                 Recommendation:
                 We recommend that the FDOT implement a procedure to ensure that subsequent
                 allocations of this nature are based on relative values of the parcels acquired.
                 Additionally, the FDOT should credit the applicable Federal project for the amount
                 overbilled.


                 Management’s Response and Corrective Action Plan:
                 We have reviewed all the documentation and discussed the exchange of parcels with the
                 District Right of Way Office. The district concurs that the cash payment was not
                 properly allocated between the four (4) parcels.

                 The usual technique in cases such as this is to allocate the cash payment based upon a
                 pro-rata comparison of the appraised values of the acquired parcels, or in the case of an
                 administrative settlement, based upon a pro-rata comparison of the settlement values of
                 the acquired parcels.

                 Because there was an administrative settlement involved in this exchange, the correct
                 method in this situation would have been to allocate the cash payment based upon the
                 relative settlement values for the four (4) parcels involved.

                 Within the next 30 days corrected invoices will be processed. Within 180 days a
                 procedure will be prepared to ensure future allocations are based on relative values of
                 the parcels acquired.
