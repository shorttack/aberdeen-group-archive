# Auditor General reports — mirrored copies

Downloaded August 21, 2026 from the Florida Auditor General's public site. All eight are freely published; none required a records request. Mirrored here because a link to a government document is not preservation — the rule the ITN 03F12GC1 404 established for this archive.

## The FLORIDA System series

Successive information technology operational audits of the same system, by the same office, across eight years. Read as a series they are a longitudinal record of the as-built machine that no single document supplies.

**Why these matter.** 2013-005 is contemporaneous with ITN 03F12GC1 and is the nearest surviving substitute for the description of the as-built system the dead ITN link used to support. It also establishes that the Child Support Enforcement Component was removed from the FLORIDA System in January 2012 and that the system is housed and operated at the Northwood Shared Resource Center. 2017-009 describes the ACCESS Management System as a "Web front-end application to the FLORIDA System mainframe" — the archive's claim that ACCESS was a wrapper rather than a replacement, in the auditor's own words.

**Caution.** None of the five has yet been read in full. The figures above come from targeted extraction, not a complete reading. Nothing from them should be cited until the relevant report is read whole.
