Suspension of the Requirement to Certify that the Vendor does not have Business Operations in Cuba or Syria

The 2012 amendment of Section 287.135(2), Florida Statutes, (§2, Ch. 2012-196, Laws of Florida) mandated a certification by a
company submitting a reply to this ITN that it does not have business operations in Cuba or Syria. However, due to pending
litigation, the Department is not permitted to require that certification as a condition of submitting a reply to this ITN. Therefore,
the Department is suspending enforcing the requirement of Section 4.2.3.6 of the ITN that a Vendor certify that it does not have
business operations in Cuba or Syria and those portions of the certification in Appendix IV.e. and the corresponding checklist
item under Appendix V of the ITN referring to business operations in Cuba or Syria.
Therefore, a Reply containing a certification under Appendix IV.e. to the ITN in either of the following forms will be acceptable as
an alternative to Appendix IV.e. of the ITN in its original form:


e. Certification Regarding Scrutinized Companies List
I, [Enter Name of Authorized Representative], as the authorized representative of [Enter Legal Name of Vendor], (the “company”)
hereby certify that, the company has reviewed Section 287.135, Florida Statutes, and certify the company is not listed on either
the Scrutinized Companies with Activities in Sudan List or the Scrutinized Companies with Activities in the Iran Petroleum Energy
Sector List.
I understand that section 287.135, Florida Statutes, prohibits Florida state agencies from contracting for goods or services over
$1,000,000, with companies on either List and that pursuant to section 287.135, Florida Statutes, the submission of a false
certification may subject the Company to civil penalties, attorney’s fees, and other penalties and consequences as provided by
section 287.135.
Signature of Authorized Representative:                                    Date:




e. Certification Regarding Scrutinized Companies List And Business Operations in Cuba/Syria
I, [Enter Name of Authorized Representative], as the authorized representative of [Enter Legal Name of Vendor], (the “company”)
hereby certify that, the company has reviewed Florida Laws Chapter 2012-196 (revising section 287.135, Florida Statutes) and
Section 287.135, Florida Statutes, and certify the company:
1. Is not listed on either the Scrutinized Companies with Activities in Sudan List or the Scrutinized Companies with Activities in
the Iran Petroleum Energy Sector List; and
2. Does not have business operations in Cuba or Syria.
I understand that section 287.135, Florida Statutes, prohibits Florida state agencies from contracting for goods or services over
$1,000,000, with companies on either List or that are engaged in business operations in Cuba or Syria, and that pursuant to
section 287.135, Florida Statutes, the submission of a false certification may subject the Company to civil penalties, attorney’s
fees, and other penalties and consequences as provided by section 287.135.
Signature of Authorized Representative:                                    Date:



A Reply containing a certification under Appendix IV.e. in its original form remains acceptable.
Nevertheless, if at a later stage in the pending litigation the Department is permitted to require the certification, Vendors will be
informed that submission of the certification contained in Appendix IV.e. in its original form will then be required and the time for
submission will be specified. It is anticipated that vendors that had previously submitted the Appendix IV.e. in its original form
will not be required to make an additional submission.
