                 This letter is in response to the Freedom of Information Act (FOIA) request, dated
                 July 28, 2026. We received your request on that date and assigned it FOIA Tracking
                 Number 2026-FNS-07231-F.

                 In your request, you sought the following:

                 Records concerning the State of Florida’s integrated public assistance eligibility
                 system (the “FLORIDA system”), procured by the Florida Department of Health
                 and Rehabilitative Services under Request for Proposal 88-74-BC issued May 20,
                 1988 and contracted to EDS Federal Corporation on May 15, 1989, for the period
                 January 1, 1986 through December 31, 1996:

                 1. All Advance Planning Documents and APD Updates submitted by Florida to FNS
                 under 7 C.F.R. § 277.18 for the FLORIDA system, with all attachments — including
                 the requirements analysis, the alternatives analysis, the cost-benefit analysis, the
                 implementation plan, and any acceptance criteria, performance standards, system
                 capacity projections, or response-time specifications contained in or attached to
                 those submissions.

                 2. All FNS approval, conditional approval, disapproval, or suspension letters
                 responding to those submissions, and any conditions imposed relating to system
                 performance.

                 3. All FNS reviews, assessments, functional demonstrations, certification reviews, or
                 post-implementation reports concerning FLORIDA system functionality, capacity,
                 or performance.

                 4. Records concerning food stamp payment error-rate liabilities assessed against
                 Florida for fiscal years 1989–90 through 1994–95 — including the FY1990–91
                 assessment reported at approximately $5 million against an error rate of 10.89%
                 versus a 10.31% national average — together with any state appeals, waivers, good-
                 cause claims, reinvestment agreements, or settlements.

Your request has been processed under the FOIA, 5 U.S.C. § 552. A search for responsive

You may submit your request to the applicable state agency in Florida, which would technically
be the place where the records you are seeking originated and are maintained.

This concludes our response to you. You have the right to appeal this determination. You may
appeal by writing to the Administrator, Food and Nutrition Administration, at 5601 Sunnyside
Avenue, Beltsville, MD 20705, or by email to SM.fn.APPEALS-FNA@usda.gov within 90
calendar days from the date of this letter. The phrase “FOIA APPEAL” should be placed in
capital letters on the front of the envelope or in the subject of the email containing the appeal.

The appeal letter should include the FOIA Tracking Number listed above, a copy of the
original request, FNA FOIA’s response to your original request, and a statement explaining the
basis of your appeal.

You may seek dispute resolution services from FNA’s FOIA Public Liaison, Ms. Evlyn Hearne.
Ms. Hearne may be contacted by telephone at (703) 305-2179 or e-mail at SM.fn.LIAISONS-
FNA@usda.gov.

You also have the option to seek dispute resolution assistance from the Office of Government
Information Services (OGIS). The Open Government Act of 2007 created OGIS within the
National Archives and Records Administration when Congress amended the FOIA. OGIS
provides mediation of FOIA disputes between appellants and Federal agencies. Participation in
mediation does not affect your right to judicial review. Contact information for OGIS can be
located at: www.Archives.gov/ogis.

If you have any questions pertaining to this matter you may contact us by email at FOIA-
