# Release v2.2.3 — FLORIDA epilogue, DCF replacement findings, and the off-GitHub register

**November 23, 2026**

A content release. It ships the FLORIDA work of November 23, 2026 and publishes the register of archival files kept off GitHub. Per the archive's sweep rule, new evidence is logged in `FLORIDA/PENDING-CHANGES.md` and applied to the papers in scheduled sweeps. The one paper edit in this release was made on Kastner's direction and is recorded as applied (row 121).

## Paper revised

- **The FLORIDA System: EDS, IBM, and the Collapse of a Landmark State Procurement** — new § 9, "Epilogue: a system with the life of a building." It records Kastner's observation, labeled as his, that a statewide eligibility system has the service life of a building's mechanical plant rather than of a procurement. The section sets that observation against the 2012 replacement record and states where the evidence stops. A revision note was added. PENDING-CHANGES row 121, applied November 23, 2026.

## Findings added

- **`FLORIDA/DCF-ITN-03F12GC1-FINDINGS.md`** — findings from DCF records request P257878-072926 on ITN 03F12GC1, *ACCESS Florida System Replacement* (2012–2013): the ITN, the Response to Inquiries, Addenda 1–15, the shortlist, and the intent-to-award record. The sources are born-digital, so quotations come from the publisher's text layer, not OCR.

## The pending-changes register

`FLORIDA/PENDING-CHANGES.md` now has 48 pending items. New since v2.2.2:

- **Rows 122–124:** facts from the 2012 Q&A and the batch 03 addenda. They cover help-desk backlog, benchmarks not prepared, performance measures not tracked, the data dictionary date, the remediate-or-replace clause, award-date slippage, and the scale and staffing requirements.
- **Row 125:** the ADLS–FLORIDA contrast drawn from *USM Corp. v. Arthur D. Little Systems*, 28 Mass. App. Ct. 108 (1989). It will go into the main report § 7 as a contained section at the next sweep, on Kastner's direction.
- **Row 126:** an open item asking whether either party in *EDS v. HRS* relied on the USM decision.
- **Row 127:** corrections, from the court record, to the separate ADLS paper *Trial and Error: The ADLS Years, 1972–1979*. They are limited to the USM litigation. The corrected draft is held outside the repository until it is adopted.

## Off-GitHub register (new)

- **`OFF-GITHUB-MANIFEST.md` / `.csv`** — a read-only register of archival files (PDF, Office, ZIP, audio and video) whose bytes are not in a GitHub release. It lists 50 distinct files, 1.36 GB, at 108 locations, with SHA-256 and copy counts. It does not assert backup status.
- **`scripts/off_github_manifest_v1.py`** — the script that generates the register.
- **`releases/MANIFEST_v2.2.2.md` / `.sha256`** — the v2.2.2 content manifest that the register checks against.
